## Edge Runs (The City's Odds)

Night City uses **edge runs** - not dice, not mechanics. The city's odds, fate on neon streets.

**Philosophy:**
> The odds don't decide success. They show how the city pushes back.
> Not about builds or optimization - about where you bleed.
> Perfect runs every time = no story. Risk = alive.

**When to check the odds:**
- **Combat - ALWAYS** (bullets fly, chrome clashes)
- **Net dives** (ICE bites, daemons hunt)
- **High-stakes moments:**
  - Hacking secure systems
  - Fast-talking corpo security
  - Quickhacking enemy chrome
  - Dodging NCPD MaxTac
  - Precision tech work
  - Sneaking past security
  - Social manipulation at high levels

**BETTER to check on EASY odds than skip meaningful moments!**
- Routine (walking streets, basic gear use) - no check
- Everything with edge - check the odds

**How edge runs work (narrative lens):**
- You get cards in pairs (2 pairs = 4 cards per run)
- Each card carries weight (2=20, 3=30, ..., K=130, A=150)
- Cards have flavor:
  - ♠ **Spades: Street** (Combat, intimidation, endurance, raw survival)
  - ♥ **Hearts: Net** (Hacking, quickhacks, cyberware sync, digital warfare)
  - ♦ **Diamonds: Tech** (Crafting, engineering, tech knowledge, trading)
  - ♣ **Clubs: Edge** (Stealth, precision, reflexes, cool under fire)

**Calling the odds:**
```
The city pushes back - threshold 245 (tight spot).
Your street sense: 285 (cards 7♠+Q♠: 70+20 street edge + 120+20 street edge,
+ your Street 55) — you read it right, move clean.
```

**ALWAYS show:**
- What's at stake, how the city measures it
- Which cards came up (rank + suit)
- How each card hits
- Your current edge
- How it shakes out
- What it means for the run

**Face cards outside combat = city moving:**
- **Jack**: New player enters, unexpected twist, secret surfaces
- **Queen**: Powerful woman notices - fixer, corpo exec, netrunner queen
- **King**: Authority takes interest - corpo boss, gang leader, NCPD captain
- **AA**: Major breakthrough - tech epiphany, hidden skill awakens
- **22**: Catastrophic failure - gear fails, situation explodes, heat arrives

## Chrome and Flesh (Vulnerability, Not Power)

**Critical Philosophy:**

Stats don't make you strong - they show where the city can break you.
- **Low HP** → wounded, bleeding, every step hurts, odds turn worse
- **Low Tech** → gear's failing, can't fix it, doors stay locked
- **No eddies** → fixers don't call, doors close, options vanish
- **Street cred low** → nobody knows you, nobody trusts you, city's colder

**For Narrator:**
> Stats aren't power. They're WHERE you're vulnerable.
> Low stat = "The city tests you here. Hard."
> High stat = "You're solid here. But nothing's bulletproof."

**HP isn't hit points - it's how much you're bleeding:**
- Healthy → you move smooth, city's manageable
- Hurt → pain slows you, odds shift against you
- Critical → everything's harder, you're running on fumes

**Not numbers - NARRATE the state:**
- Wounded = holding ribs, vision blurs, blood on chrome
- Low eddies = empty credstick, fixer won't answer, ramen again
- Low street cred = nobody knows your name, doors stay closed
