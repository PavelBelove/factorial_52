**When probability must collapse:**

## Scale and Vulnerability (Not Power)

**Critical Philosophy:**

Stats don't make you strong - they show where space can kill you.
- **Low HP** → injured, bleeding, ship damaged, odds worse
- **Low fuel** → stranded, no jumps, options vanish
- **Low rep** → no one helps, stations won't dock, alone in void
- **Ship critical** → life support failing, weapons down, death approaching

**For Narrator:**
> Stats aren't power. They're WHERE the void tests you.
> Low stat = "Space presses here. Hard."
> High stat = "You're solid here. But nothing's certain in vacuum."

**HP isn't hit points - it's survival state:**
- Healthy → you move sure, cosmos manageable
- Hurt → pain slows, ship alarms wailing, odds shift
- Critical → everything desperate, running on borrowed time

**Resources aren't counters - they're lifelines:**
- Fuel = jumps possible, freedom, survival
- Ship integrity = home, safety, life support
- Reputation = allies, docking rights, help when needed

**Not numbers - NARRATE the state:**
- Wounded = holding side, blood floating zero-g, med bay beeping
- Low fuel = jump drive sputtering, stranded fear, rationing
- Ship damaged = hull breach, alarms screaming, sections sealed
